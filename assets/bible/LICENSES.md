# Bundled Bible text

Every translation in this folder ships inside the app and is readable with no
network and no account. Each is here because its licence permits that.

## kjv — King James Version

Public domain. Generated by `scripts/build-bible.mjs` from
[aruljohn/Bible-kjv](https://github.com/aruljohn/Bible-kjv).

## twi-akuapem — Biblica® Open Akuapem Twi Contemporary Bible

## twi-asante — Biblica® Open Asante Twi Contemporary Bible

Copyright © 1996, 2020 Biblica, Inc. Released under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

---

The Twi editions were imported by `scripts/import-vpl-bible.mjs` from
[eBible.org](https://ebible.org), which publishes the same Biblica editions as
verse-per-line text.

**CC BY-SA obliges us to two things.** The text is copied verbatim — no
edits, no re-punctuation — and the attribution line for each translation is
displayed in the app beside the text it belongs to, from the `copyright` field
in `constants/bible-translations.ts`.

"Biblica" is a registered trademark of Biblica, Inc. The licence permits
redistribution of the text; it does not grant use of the trademark, which is
why the app names these translations without claiming any endorsement.
