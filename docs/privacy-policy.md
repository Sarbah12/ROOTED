# Privacy Policy for Rooted

**Last updated: 17 August 2026**

Rooted is a Bible study app published by Accra Resource Center LBG. This policy
explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. It describes
the app as it actually behaves — if something here stops being true, the policy
is wrong and should be corrected rather than the behaviour excused.

---

## The short version

- **You can read the entire Bible without an account.** No sign-up, no email, no
  data collected.
- **We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.** There is no
  third-party SDK in the app watching what you do.
- **We never sell or share your personal information.**
- **You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app**, at
  any time, without asking us.

---

## What we collect

### If you never sign in

Nothing leaves your device. Reading, searching, highlighting and the verse of
the day all work offline, and what you do is stored only on your phone.

### If you create an account

**What you give us when you sign up**

- A display name
- One of: an email address, a phone number, or a username
- A password, which is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is never
  visible to us

If you sign up with a username or phone number rather than an email, we create
an internal address for it (`yourname@users.rootedbible.app`). It is not a real
mailbox and cannot receive mail.

**What you create in the app**

- Study notes and sermon notes, including any preacher, church, series and date
  you record
- Prayer requests and whether you have marked them answered
- Study plans you create or join, the days you complete, and your reflections
- Blog posts, comments and likes
- Quiz results and reading progress
- Your settings — theme, text size, reminder time, notification choices

**What we do not collect**

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, health data, or
advertising identifiers. The app does not ask for these permissions.

---

## How your information is used

Only to make the app work for you: to sign you in, to sync your notes and
prayers between your devices, to show your plans to the people you share them
with, and to send the emails described below.

We do not profile you, build advertising audiences, or run automated
decision-making on your data.

---

## What is visible to other people

Most of what you write is private to your account. These are the exceptions,
and each one is a deliberate action you take:

| What | Who can see it |
|---|---|
| A study plan you make public | Anyone using Rooted |
| A study plan shared by code | Anyone you give the code to |
| Your progress and display name inside a plan | Other members of that plan |
| Reflections you post on a plan day | Other members of that plan |
| A blog post you publish | Anyone using Rooted |
| Comments and likes | Anyone who can see the post |

**Your notes, prayers, quiz results and highlights are never shown to anyone
else.**

---

## Services we rely on

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Google Firebase Authentication | Your sign-in identifier and password | To sign you in securely |
| Our own server and database | The account data described above | To store and sync your content |
| Resend | Your email address | To send password-reset and welcome emails |
| Tyndale House (api.nlt.to) | Only the passage you are reading | To show the New Living Translation |
| API.Bible | Only the passage you are reading | To show other licensed translations |

The Bible services receive a passage reference and nothing else. They are not
told who you are.

---

## Where your data is kept

On your device, and on our server and database, which are hosted in the United
States. If you use Rooted from outside the United States, your information is
transferred and stored there.

---

## Keeping it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete
your account it is removed immediately and permanently — see below.

---

## Your choices

**Delete your account.** In the app: Settings → Delete my account. This removes
your profile, notes, prayers, plans, reflections, posts, comments, quiz results
and settings. It cannot be undone, and we do not keep a copy.

**Turn off notifications.** Settings → Notifications, or in your device's own
settings.

**Sign out.** Settings → Sign out. Reading stays available.

**Ask us anything about your data**, including a copy of it, by writing to the
address below.

---

## Children

Rooted is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us
and we will delete it.

---

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and note
the change in the app. Continuing to use Rooted after a change means you accept
the updated policy.
